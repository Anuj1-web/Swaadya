Our Beginning

Starting small. Preparing carefully.

Swaadya began with a simple idea: everyday cooking deserves spices that feel fresh, honest and full of flavour.

Instead of starting with a large catalogue, we are starting with three everyday spices — red chilli, turmeric and coriander.

See Our Process

Why Swaadya?

At Swaadya, we believe everyday spices should taste like real spices.

We aim to prepare products in small batches, keep the process simple and transparent, and gradually build Swaadya through the trust of our customers.

We are starting small, learning from every order, and improving with every batch.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What spices do you currently sell?
Red chilli, turmeric and coriander powder.
Are your spices freshly prepared?
We prepare our products in small batches to maintain freshness and flavour.
What sizes are available?
250g and 500g initially.
How should I store the spices?
Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and moisture. Keep the pack properly sealed after opening.
Where do you deliver?
Initially Jaipur/Rajasthan and other serviceable locations. Exact availability depends on the delivery PIN code.
How can I contact you?
Use the Contact section below or WhatsApp.
