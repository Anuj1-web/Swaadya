Our Beginning

Starting small. Preparing carefully.

Swaadya began with a simple idea: everyday cooking deserves spices that feel fresh, honest and full of flavour.

Instead of starting with a large catalogue, we are starting with three everyday spices — red chilli, turmeric and coriander.

See Our Process

Why Swaadya?

At Swaadya, we believe everyday spices should taste like real spices.

We aim to prepare products in small batches, keep the process simple and transparent, and gradually build Swaadya through the trust of our customers.

We are starting small, learning from every order, and improving with every batch.

Refund / Cancellation

We want every Swaadya order to reach you in good condition. This policy explains how cancellations, refunds and payment-related issues are handled.

Last updated: 11 August 2026

1. Order Cancellation

Cancellation requests should be submitted as soon as possible after placing an order. Once an order has entered processing, preparation or dispatch, cancellation may no longer be possible.

2. Cancellation Before Processing

If an eligible cancellation is accepted before preparation or dispatch, the applicable refund will be initiated to the original payment method.

3. Payment Failure

If your payment fails but an amount appears to have been debited, the transaction will be checked against the payment provider's transaction status.

4. Duplicate Payment

If you believe you have been charged more than once for the same order, contact Swaadya with the relevant transaction details so the payment can be verified.

5. Damaged or Incorrect Products

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, contact us promptly after delivery with your order details and supporting photographs where applicable.

6. Refund Processing

Once a refund is approved, it will be initiated through the applicable payment method. The time taken for the amount to appear in your account may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

7. Food and Spice Products

For food and spice products, returns may be restricted for hygiene and safety reasons except where applicable law or Swaadya's stated product policy provides otherwise.

8. Contact for Refunds

For cancellation or refund assistance, contact Swaadya customer support with your order number and relevant details.
